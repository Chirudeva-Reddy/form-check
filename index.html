What the numbers mean

Each figure comes from joint angles that a single camera can measure reasonably well. Things it can't see, like bar load or knee rotation, are left out.

End range and range of motion
The joint angle at the deepest or highest point, and how far it travelled from your start position. In a 2021 meta-analysis of 16 studies, full-range training built more strength and more leg muscle than partial reps. Deep squats built more front-thigh muscle than shallow ones in one trial, and more glute and adductor muscle in another. Pallarés 2021, Bloomquist 2013, Kubo 2019
Lower and lift time
How long the eccentric (lowering) and concentric (lifting) halves of the rep took. Muscle growth was similar for whole reps lasting anywhere from 0.5 to 8 seconds, so the page only asks you to slow down when a whole rep takes less than about half a second. Schoenfeld 2015, Wilk 2021
Lift speed
Average joint speed during the lifting half, compared with your fastest rep in the set. In lab studies, the drop in bar speed across a set tracks fatigue closely. Sets stopped at 20% and at 40% loss both built strength, and the 40% sets added more muscle. Sánchez-Medina 2011, González-Badillo 2017, Pareja-Blanco 2017
Torso lean
How far your trunk tips forward from vertical, measured side-on. Some lean is normal in a squat. When lifters were stopped from letting their knees travel forward, they leaned more and hip torque rose about tenfold. Fry 2003, Schoenfeld 2010
Left and right gap
The difference between your two arms at end range, measured from the front. Studies disagree on how much side-to-side differences matter, and in a study of knee angles from 2D video a change had to pass 7.5 to 9 degrees to be real. The page flags gaps above 15 degrees, about twice that. Munro 2012, Bishop 2018
How far to trust it
Open-source pose models measured hip and knee angles during walking to within about 4 to 5 degrees of lab motion capture, and MediaPipe tracked joint angles about as well as a Kinect depth camera. Lift speed here is a joint angle speed, not the bar speed those velocity studies measured, so compare it with your own reps and not with published numbers. Washabaugh 2022, Lafayette 2022
